Ladepeche Belote: 2v2 Real-Time French Card Engine

Developed a server-authoritative multiplayer Belote card platform with AI bot decision logic, complex trick-taking move validation, 82-point contract math, and headless integration into an international gaming portal via Gamify APIs.

Game Mode
Solo (with AI Bots), Multiplayer 2v2 & Tournaments
Integration
Headless Game Service via Gamify Engine APIs
Card Engine
32-Card French Belote with Trump Rules
Stack
Node.js, Socket.IO, Redis, MongoDB

1. Game Overview & What is Belote?

Belote is one of the most popular trick-taking card games in France and continental Europe. Played by four players formed into two opposing partnerships (Team North/South vs. Team East/West), the game uses a 32-card deck (cards 7 through Ace).

The objective is for the partnership that takes the contract to score at least 82 points out of 162 total available points by strategically choosing the trump suit, following suit rules, overtrumping opponents, and managing bonus declarations.

2. Headless Third-Party Portal Integration (Gamify API)

This system was engineered as an autonomous game service microservice for an international media and gaming portal. Rather than maintaining user registrations and bank balances independently:

  • When a player launches Belote from the client's web portal, our server authenticates the launch token, queries the client's Gamify API for user metadata, wallet balances, and active session tokens.
  • Throughout the game, all balance deductions, tournament fees, and winning prize payouts are communicated in real time back to the client's central gaming engine.
  • This headless separation allowed the client to offer an authentic Belote experience while retaining total control over their user base, KYC, and central wallet architecture.

3. Complete Hand Lifecycle & State Workflow

# Belote Round Execution State Machine 1. [Room Matchmaking & Seating] ├── 4 Real Players or Solo Mode (Empty seats filled by AI bots) └── Assign Dealer Position & Initialize Round │ 2. [Initial Deal & Retourne Card] ├── Deal 5 cards to each of the 4 players └── Reveal 1 face-up card: The "Retourne Card" │ 3. [Trump Selection Bidding Phase] ├── Round 1: Players decide in clockwise order: Take Retourne Suit or Pass └── Round 2 (If all pass): Pick any of remaining 3 suits or Pass └── Redeal: If all 4 pass both rounds ➔ Discard cards & redeal new hand │ 4. [Remaining Deal & Trick Playing] ├── Deal remaining 3 cards (8 cards total in hand) ├── 8 successive tricks (4 cards played per trick) ├── Server validates every card: Must follow suit; mandatory overtrump └── Determine Trick Winner ➔ Collect trick ➔ Lead next trick │ 5. [Round Score Evaluation] ├── Calculate Trump vs. Non-Trump Card Points ├── Add "Dix de Der" (+10 bonus points for winning final 8th trick) ├── Validate Belote / Rebelote (+20 bonus points for King & Queen of Trump) ├── Contract Validation: Did taking team reach >= 82 points? │ ├── Success: Both teams keep their respective points │ └── Failure (Dedans): Opposing team receives all 162 points! └── Check Match Target: First team to exceed 501 points wins match

4. Trump Hierarchy vs. Non-Trump Hierarchy

Belote features a dynamic card valuation model depending on whether a card matches the chosen trump suit:

Card Trump Suit Point Value Non-Trump Suit Point Value
Jack (J) 20 Points (Highest Trump) 2 Points
Nine (9) 14 Points (2nd Highest Trump) 0 Points
Ace (A) 11 Points 11 Points (Highest Non-Trump)
Ten (10) 10 Points 10 Points
King (K) 4 Points 4 Points
Queen (Q) 3 Points 3 Points
8 & 7 0 Points 0 Points

5. AI Bot Decision Engine & Reconnection Recovery

  • Intelligent Bot Bidding: Bots evaluate their 5 initial cards, computing a weighted strength score based on counts of Jacks, 9s, and Aces in the Retourne suit. Only hands with sufficient point potential trigger an affirmative bid.
  • Card Selection Logic: During trick play, the bot calculates optimal play: discarding low-value cards if their human partner is already winning the trick, or playing a winning trump if an opponent currently holds trick authority.
  • Zero-Loss Reconnection: If a player experiences internet loss, the server maintains the player's seat and hand state in MongoDB & Redis. When the client reconnects via Socket.IO, the active round state, cards in hand, current trump, and trick history are restored instantly without forfeiture.
