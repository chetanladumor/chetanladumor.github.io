Swisspoker Multiplayer Platform: Texas Hold'em & MTT Architecture

A high-concurrency real-time poker ecosystem built with Node.js, Socket.IO, Redis, and MongoDB, featuring server-authoritative game state, dynamic multi-tier side pots, Pokersolver showdowns, MTT dynamic table rebalancing, and hierarchical rake commissions.

1. Executive Summary & System Scope

Poker is one of the most state-sensitive, mathematically rigorous real-time gaming architectures. Unlike casual applications, poker demands absolute server-authoritative integrity: client devices are untrusted renderers, and all card shuffles, deals, betting limits, and pot divisions must occur strictly on the backend.

The backend manages over 50 distinct database models encompassing player wallets, chips, financial transactions, live tables, tournaments, blind structures, club affiliations, agent revenue trees, and compliance audits.

2. Supported Variants & Hand Evaluation

The platform supports two major international poker game formats:

  • Texas Hold'em: 2 private hole cards dealt per player, followed by 5 community board cards (Flop, Turn, River). Players form the highest ranking 5-card combination from any mix of hole and board cards.
  • Omaha: 4 private hole cards dealt per player. Crucially, players must use exactly 2 hole cards and exactly 3 community board cards to form their final hand.

At the Showdown stage, the backend leverages an optimized Pokersolver evaluation algorithm to rank hands from Royal Flush down to High Card, resolving tie-breakers and kicker card comparisons instantaneously.

3. Cash Game Hand Lifecycle & State Machine

The table state machine executes server-authoritatively across five distinct poker phases, guaranteeing transactional fairness:

Phase 01

Seat Reservation & Buy-In Ingress

< 50ms Turnaround
👛 Wallet Validation

Validates player wallet balance against table minimums; atomically debits user balance and credits table chip stack.

🪑 Seat Assignment

Allocates table seat position in Redis table state and broadcasts seat occupancy event to all seated players.

↓
Phase 02

Dealer Button Rotation & Cryptographic Deal

Deterministic Fortuna PRNG
🔄 Position Tracking

Rotates Dealer Button, Small Blind (SB), and Big Blind (BB) positions; collects mandatory forced bets to seed main pot.

🃏 Card Secrecy

Deck is shuffled server-side via Fortuna PRNG. Private hole cards (2 for Hold'em, 4 for Omaha) are delivered strictly to the owning player socket.

↓
Phase 03

Sequential Betting Rounds (Pre-Flop ➔ Flop ➔ Turn ➔ River)

30-Second Authoritative Timer
⏱️ Action Countdown

Server-side timer ticks per active seat. If turn expires without input, server forces auto-check or fold.

🛡️ Move Validation

Validates player action (Fold, Check, Call, Raise, All-In); updates round bets and resolves pot eligibility sets.

🌐 Board Card Reveal

When betting rounds complete, reveals Flop (3 cards), Turn (1 card), and River (1 card) simultaneously across sockets.

↓
Phase 04

Showdown, Pokersolver & Side Pot Distribution

Instantaneous Hand Resolution
🧠 Hand Evaluation

Evaluates active 5-card hands using Pokersolver, ranking hands from Royal Flush to High Card with automated kicker comparisons.

📐 Side Pot Settlement

Evaluates multi-tier side pots in descending stack sequence, ensuring uneven all-in stacks are settled equitably.

📊 Rake & Ledger Log

Deducts table rake commission according to club caps, credits winner chip balance, and records hand replay in MongoDB.

4. Deep Dive: Dynamic Side Pot Management

One of the most complex algorithmic requirements in poker is handling multiple simultaneous all-ins with uneven chip stacks.

📐 The Side Pot Algorithm

Scenario: Player A has 100 chips, Player B has 500 chips, and Player C has 1,000 chips. All three go all-in.

1. Main Pot (300 chips): 100 chips from Player A, 100 from Player B, and 100 from Player C. All three players are eligible to win this pot.
2. Side Pot 1 (800 chips): Remaining 400 from Player B and matched 400 from Player C. Only Player B and Player C are eligible.
3. Side Pot 2 (500 chips): Remaining 500 from Player C uncontested, refunded back to Player C immediately.

The engine sorts all active bets, creates discrete side-pot buckets, links player eligibility sets to each bucket, and evaluates winner hands for each pot in descending sequence.

5. Multi-Table Tournament (MTT) Architecture

The tournament subsystem powers large-scale competitive events:

  • Automated Blind Structures: Blinds escalate on strict scheduled intervals (e.g., Level 1: 25/50 ➔ Level 2: 50/100 ➔ Level 3: 100/200), forcing action and pacing tournament duration.
  • Dynamic Table Balancing: As players run out of chips and are eliminated, tables become uneven (e.g., Table 1 has 3 players while Table 2 has 8). The system executes an automated rebalancing algorithm, picking players from larger tables and transferring their seat and chip stack seamlessly between hands without disrupting ongoing action.
  • Tiered Payout Distribution: Predefined prize structures automatically calculate final settlements (e.g., 1st: 50%, 2nd: 30%, 3rd: 20%) when the final table resolves.

6. Rake Management & MLM Agent Hierarchy

To support commercial iGaming club operations, the platform implements a hierarchical rake commission engine:

  • Dynamic Rake Percentages & Caps: Deducts a configurable percentage (typically 3%–5%) from cash pots, subject to a maximum cap per hand. "No Flop, No Drop" policy enforced.
  • Agent Commission Trees (MLM): Commissions flow automatically through multi-level hierarchies: Master Agent ➔ Super Agent ➔ Sub-Agent ➔ Player. Each tier receives automated real-time rake rebates credited to their operational wallets.
