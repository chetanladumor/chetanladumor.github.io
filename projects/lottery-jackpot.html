Online Lottery & Automated Draw Engine

An automated multi-tier lottery backend supporting Pick 3, Pick 4, and Pick 5 game formats, powered by cryptographic seed generation, distributed Redis locks, and high-speed batch prize evaluation.

1. Executive Summary & Platform Architecture

Lottery engines require absolute audit compliance, immutable transaction history, and strict scheduling reliability. Unlike casual games where an occasional dropped packet can be retried, a delayed, missed, or duplicate lottery draw introduces legal liabilities.

The backend orchestrates automated draw scheduling, ticket cryptographic serialization, and prize distribution engines, enabling tens of thousands of active ticket purchases and instantaneous draw resolution.

2. The Draw Execution & Ticket Validation Flow

  • Ticket Serialization & Tamper-Proofing: Every ticket receives a cryptographic hash upon purchase, binding selected numbers, user wallet ID, timestamp, and target draw ID to prevent counterfeit claims.
  • Distributed Lock Safeguard: When scheduled draw times trigger, background worker nodes compete for a Redis distributed lock. Only the single victorious worker can execute the draw sequence, preventing split-brain double draws across clustered servers.
  • Parallel Prize Evaluation: Millions of purchased combinations are evaluated against the winning draw numbers using batch bitmask operations in memory, distributing fixed and progressive jackpot tiers into player accounts within seconds.
