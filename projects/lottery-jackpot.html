Online Lottery & Automated Draw Engine

1. Executive Summary & Platform Architecture

Lottery engines require absolute audit compliance, immutable transaction history, and strict scheduling reliability. Unlike casual games where an occasional dropped packet can be retried, a delayed, missed, or duplicate lottery draw introduces legal liabilities.

The backend orchestrates automated draw scheduling, ticket cryptographic serialization, and prize distribution engines, enabling tens of thousands of active ticket purchases and instantaneous draw resolution.

2. Complete Draw Lifecycle & State Engine

The lottery engine enforces strict audit-compliant state transitions, guaranteeing that ticket pools are locked and evaluated with zero split-brain execution across distributed workers:

Phase 01

Ticket Ingress & Cryptographic Hash Serialization

< 20ms Purchase Ack
🎟️ Ticket Purchase

Players submit single or syndicate tickets; validated against draw rules and price schedules.

🔒 SHA-256 Hash Seal

Every ticket generates an immutable hash binding numbers, user ID, timestamp, and target Draw ID.

⚡ Atomic Reservation

Debits wallet balances atomically via Redis, enqueuing ticket documents to MongoDB via bulk operations.

↓
Phase 02

Draw Lock Acquisition & Pool Freezing

Redlock Mutex < 5ms
⏰ Precision Scheduler

BullMQ cron workers trigger draw closure at the exact scheduled second, rejecting subsequent purchases.

🛡️ Redlock Mutex

Nodes compete for an exclusive Redis distributed lock; prevents split-brain duplicate draws across cluster instances.

❄️ Immutable Snapshot

Freezes the active ticket pool and transitions draw state to LOCKED_FOR_EVALUATION.

↓
Phase 03

Fortuna PRNG Ball Selection & Audit Trail

Certified Cryptographic Draw
🎲 Cryptographic Draw

Fortuna PRNG extracts winning ball sequences and bonus numbers under strict entropy seed initialization.

📜 Audit Trail Proof

Generates an HMAC digital signature of the drawn numbers, committing the hash to an unalterable audit ledger.

📡 Live Broadcast

Streams drawn numbers ball-by-ball in real-time to active player sessions and TV displays via Socket.IO.

↓
Phase 04

Bitmask Prize Matrix & Tiered Wallet Credit

Millions Checked in < 3s
⚡ Fast Bitmask Scan

Maps ticket numbers into 64-bit integer masks, matching millions of entries against drawn numbers in RAM.

🏆 Tier & Jackpot Split

Allocates fixed prizes and computes pari-mutuel progressive jackpot shares across confirmed winner brackets.

💰 Atomic Wallet Credit

Credits prize payouts directly to player wallets and triggers automated winner notification queues in RabbitMQ.
